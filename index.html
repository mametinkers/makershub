McGill manufacturing resources

Every place to make things at McGill.

Makerspaces, machine shops, 3D printers, laser cutters and fabrication services — who can use them, where they are, and how to get in. All in one place.

Popular:

New to making? Start here.

Not sure where to go? Pick what you're trying to do.

01

I need a quick 3D print

Any McGill student can send a model to the library printers, and engineering students get 200 g free at the Fishbowl. Export your part as an .stl file and submit it.

02

I want to machine parts myself

Take the Faculty of Engineering training modules (hand tools → lathe → mill). Design team members get priority.

03

I want someone to make it for me

Faculty Workshop Services' technicians can machine, weld, waterjet or laser-cut your part from a drawing and a work order.

04

I'm building electronics

ECSE students can join The Factory for free — benches, components and workshops.

FOAPAL

McGill's account number for a fund (a lab, design team or course). Shops charge work and training to it — ask your supervisor or team captain.

Work order

The form you submit to have technicians fabricate a part. It needs drawings and a fund controller's signature.

.stl file

The standard 3D-print file. In SolidWorks or Fusion: File → Save As / Export → STL.

TM #1 / #2 / #3

Engineering's machine-shop training modules: basics & hand tools, then lathe, then milling.

Know a space we're missing?
